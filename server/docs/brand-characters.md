# Brand characters (landing page)

Five FICTIONAL, stylized characters (matte clay 3D illustration). They stand for
roles in our system. They are not staff, not clients and not based on any real
person; never caption them as a team member, a client or a testimonial.

| Character | Role | Personality | Owns in the process | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maps, The Strategist | Audit, positioning, product and launch plan | Calm. Sees the whole board before moving a piece. | 1 Call, 2 Audit and strategy | Amber `#c08a2e` |
| Forge, The Builder | Products, store, checkout, funnels | Hands-on. Ships what was planned, on the date planned. | 3 We build the products | Steel blue `#3f6c8f` |
| Scope, The Growth Analyst | Launch, ads, funnels, analytics | Curious. Reads the numbers before forming an opinion. | 4 Launch | Violet `#7a62a8` |
| Pulse, The Coach-Tech | App, membership, coaching systems, customer support | Upbeat and patient. Keeps members moving. | 5 We run and optimize | Green `#4f8a63` |
| Nova, The Creator | Creator archetype (the partner) | Energetic. On camera, being themselves. | 6 Monthly profit share | Coral `#cf6a55` |

Prompts and the shared style suffix: `.landing-assets/prompts.json`.
Sheets are generated first; every pose uses its character's sheet as reference.
Output: `assets/images/landing-b2b/` (WebP, 2x rendered size).

> 2026-11-12: the characters are no longer shown on the landing page (replaced by object icons, `ico-*.webp`). The image files stay in `assets/images/landing-b2b/` unused.
